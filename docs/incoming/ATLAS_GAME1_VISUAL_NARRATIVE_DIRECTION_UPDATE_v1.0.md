## 1. Director decision — authoritative update

1. **R01 Nomad Valley is the starting point**, grounded in home, community, warmth, and the beginning of Okto's journey. Do not front-load ancient Golem skeletons or civilization-scale revelations.
2. **R02 Broken Crossing is a border/transition region**, visually darker and more dramatic than R01. Its identity is fracture, broken passage, precarious crossings, mist, damaged structures, uncertainty, and the first trust between Okto and Moss. It must not read as another lush fantasy wonderland.
3. **R03 Overgrown Aqueduct restores visual beauty and wonder** after the tension of R02: water, living ecology, light, and a renewed sense of discovery. The *end of R03* is the earliest intended stage for clear ancient-Golem remains/major Golem-history visual clues.
4. **R04 Echoing Ruins progressively reveals much more of the ancient Golem civilization**, using archaeological environment storytelling. Do not reveal the entire history immediately.
5. **Golems are genuinely part of the world's historical life/background.** The earlier caution against their existence as lore is superseded. However, specific origin, anatomy, fate, identity, and mechanisms remain governed by the story canon, not invented by concept art.
6. Maintain **Cinematic Stylized Painterly**: readable stylized gameplay silhouettes and controlled textures; cinematic lighting, atmosphere, and scale in background vistas.
7. Ancient Golem remains should look like **weathered mineral anatomy / skeletal structures integrated into geology and ecology**, not humanoid stone statues, futuristic robots, alien machinery, neon cores, or repeated sci-fi rings.
8. The Game Director's newest reveal pacing supersedes earlier concept sheets that prominently placed giant hands, skulls, ribs, or other unmistakable Golem remains in A01–A07.

## 4. Art and environment construction rules

- **Foreground/gameplay plane:** crisp silhouettes, high value separation, readable ledges, cracks and interactables at actual Godot camera scale.
- **Midground:** selective material detail, damaged bridge supports, sparse rooted vegetation, physical traces of age.
- **Background:** painterly depth, large topographic shapes, weather and atmosphere; do not let spectacle obscure traversable paths.
- **R01 materials:** local stone, aged timber, woven fabric, soil, restrained grasses and cultivated plants.
- **R02 materials:** fractured stone, damp mineral surfaces, splintered timber, rope, eroded masonry, limited moss. Darkness through value and atmosphere, not loss of gameplay visibility.
- **R02 construction language:** primitive functional engineering, not repeated Roman arches or precision sci-fi machinery.
- **R03 light:** visibly earned after R02; reserve richer greens, water sparkle and wider scenic openness.
- **Golem design when later revealed:** irregular mineral anatomy, partial skeletal forms, erosion and ecological integration. Distinguish remains from monuments constructed by inhabitants.
- **No retroactive lore invention:** environmental art may imply a past, but does not establish exact Golem origins or cause of destruction unless the canon explicitly supports it.
