## Parallel Asset Production / Integration Gates

- **Architecture/props:** Okto house, sparse cottages, communal terrace, one small bridge, modest village life.
- **Landmarks:** old tree, A02 three Survey silhouettes, A03 passage mouth and horizon frame.
- **Backgrounds/lighting:** A01 home vista, A02 obscured ridge, A03 compressed-to-cold reveal; 5-layer parallax system.
- **Gameplay/engineering:** existing Okto controller calibration, area scene skeletons, Survey trigger, camera reveal and recovery logic.

### Dependencies
All three area concept sheets and asset briefs may progress concurrently. However, export-resolution decisions depend on `G1` sprite/camera calibration. Level collision geometry depends on real jump J. Final panorama crop depends on actual camera framing. No team should finalize these before measurement.

1. `G1` controller/texture camera audit and baseline capture.
2. A01/A02/A03 greybox pass, Survey and reveal functional.

4. Integrate landmarks and P1 art in all areas; review continuity.
